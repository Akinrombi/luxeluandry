1. Terms and Conditions

These terms apply when you create a Luxeluandry account, request a quote or place a laundry order. By placing an order, you confirm that you have read these terms and the related policies. If you do not agree, do not place an order.

Our services and order acceptance

Luxeluandry provides laundry and garment-care services, which may include washing, ironing, starching, collection and delivery. A submitted request is not accepted until Luxeluandry confirms it. We may contact you to clarify garment details, service requirements, collection arrangements or pricing before accepting an order.

Customer information and instructions

You must provide accurate contact details, service selections, item quantities and pickup/delivery information. Tell us about delicate fabrics, special care labels, existing damage, colour-transfer risks, difficult stains and any instructions that could affect cleaning. Remove cash, jewellery, documents and other valuables from pockets before handover.

Service timeframes

Available choices may include urgent service, 2–3 days and within one week. These are estimated service windows, not guaranteed completion times unless we expressly confirm a guaranteed date in writing. The estimate may depend on workload, garment type, stain treatment, weather, equipment or other circumstances. If a material delay becomes apparent, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you and agree a revised arrangement.

Prices and changes

Prices shown at checkout are based on the items, quantities and service selected. The total may include an urgent-service fee or other disclosed charge. We will seek your approval before applying an additional charge or changing the agreed service. Do not rely on a price communicated outside the website unless it is confirmed by an authorised Luxeluandry representative.

Customer accounts

Keep your password confidential and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account unless the law provides otherwise. We may restrict access where reasonably necessary to protect customers, the service or the website, while respecting applicable law.

2. Privacy Policy

Luxeluandry processes personal information in accordance with applicable Nigerian data-protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Information we may collect

  • Identity and contact details, such as name, email address and phone number.
  • Pickup and delivery addresses and instructions.
  • Account details, order history, garment descriptions and customer-support messages.
  • Payment references, payment status, transaction amount and related records. Card credentials and authentication are handled by the payment provider; we do not ask you to send your PIN or one-time password to staff.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website, such as device/browser information, log records and necessary session data.

Purposes and lawful basis

We use information to create accounts, provide laundry services, arrange collection and delivery, process and reconcile payments, communicate order updates, answer questions, prevent fraud, secure the service and meet legal/accounting obligations. Depending on the activity, processing may be necessary to perform a contract, comply with a legal obligation, protect legitimate interests or rely on your consent where required.

Service providers and disclosures

We may share the minimum information reasonably needed with providers that support the service, including Supabase (database/authentication), Render (website hosting), Paystack (payment processing), and authorised collection/delivery personnel. We may disclose information to a regulator, law-enforcement body or other party where required or permitted by law. We do not sell personal information.

International processing

Some service providers or their infrastructure may process or store information outside Nigeria. Where applicable, Luxeluandry will take steps required by Nigerian data-protection law for international transfers, including appropriate safeguards.

Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes, including legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and dispute-resolution needs. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion where appropriate, restriction or objection to certain processing, and portability where applicable. You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing; withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out. Some records may need to be retained to comply with law or resolve disputes. Contact us using the details below. You may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where you believe your rights have not been respected.

Children

Our online account and ordering service is intended for adults. A parent or legal guardian should contact us if a child's information has been submitted without appropriate authority.

Cookies and similar technologies

We may use strictly necessary session or security technologies to keep you signed in and make the website work. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain consent where required.

3. Refund and Cancellation Policy

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel or change an order. We will confirm whether work has started and what options are available.

  • Before work begins: If the order has not been processed or cleaning started, cancellation and a refund will generally be available, subject to any lawful, clearly disclosed costs already incurred.
  • After work begins: If cleaning or other services have started, any amount retained will reflect work actually performed and reasonable costs, subject always to applicable consumer rights.
  • Service unavailable or cancelled by us: If Luxeluandry cannot provide a paid service, we will offer an appropriate refund or an alternative with your agreement.
  • Duplicate or incorrect payment: Contact us with the transaction reference. Confirmed duplicate or excess payments will be refunded through an appropriate payment channel.
  • Refund timing: We will initiate an approved refund promptly. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment provider and the customer's bank.

We will not impose a blanket “no refunds in all circumstances” rule. Nothing in this policy excludes any refund or other remedy required by law.

4. Clothing Care, Damage, Loss and Claims

We will take reasonable care of garments while they are in our possession or control and use cleaning methods appropriate to the information available and the service agreed.

Before cleaning

Please disclose fabric composition where known, care-label instructions, existing tears, missing buttons, colour instability, embellishments, prior repairs and stains. We may photograph items at intake to record their condition. Where a garment presents a material cleaning risk, we will try to explain the risk and obtain your instruction before proceeding.

Stains and delicate items

We cannot promise that every stain will be removed. Some stains become permanent over time or may react unpredictably to treatment. Certain fabrics, dyes, trims or prior treatments may be sensitive even when care instructions are followed. We will not knowingly proceed with a materially risky treatment without seeking your instruction where reasonably practicable.

Reporting a problem

Please inspect your garments after return and report missing or damaged items as soon as reasonably possible, preferably within 48 hours, with your order number, a description and photographs where available. This requested reporting period helps us investigate while details are fresh; it does not remove any legal rights or time limits that apply.

Investigation and fair remedy

We will review intake records, photographs, cleaning notes, delivery records and other relevant evidence. If Luxeluandry is responsible, we will discuss a fair remedy based on the circumstances and applicable law. Depending on the case, this may include repair, replacement, refund or reasonable compensation. Any assessment will consider the garment's condition, age, use, value and evidence, without automatically imposing an unlawful cap or excluding liability that cannot legally be excluded.

We are not responsible for a pre-existing defect or a risk that could not reasonably have been identified or avoided, except to the extent our own act or omission contributed to the loss or damage. This clause does not excuse negligence or override statutory consumer rights.

5. Pickup and Delivery Policy

Customers must provide a complete and accurate address, a working phone number and any access instructions. Collection and delivery times are arranged with the customer and may be subject to availability, traffic, weather, access restrictions and other operational conditions.

  • Please make the garments available at the agreed collection time.
  • If you will not be available, contact us as early as possible to rearrange.
  • Additional delivery attempts or address changes may involve a charge only where the charge is disclosed and agreed in advance.
  • We will take reasonable steps to deliver to you or a recipient you have authorised. Tell us in advance if another person will receive the garments.
  • We will record completion of collection/delivery where reasonably practicable.

We will notify you of significant delays and work with you to arrange a reasonable alternative. Responsibility for garments will be assessed according to when and how they were handed over and the applicable law.

6. Customer Complaints and Dispute Resolution

We welcome complaints and will handle them respectfully and fairly. Please provide your name, order number, contact details, a clear description of the issue and any supporting photographs or payment evidence.

We aim to acknowledge complaints within 2 business days and provide an update or proposed resolution within 10 business days. If an investigation needs longer, we will explain why and give you an update. These are service targets, not a restriction on your legal rights.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may ask for the matter to be reviewed by the business owner/manager. You may also approach the appropriate Nigerian consumer-protection or data-protection authority, or seek independent legal advice.

7. Payments, Account and Website Security

  • Paystack or another payment provider processes online payments. Luxeluandry records payment references and status to manage orders and receipts.
  • Never share your card PIN, banking password or OTP with anyone claiming to be Luxeluandry support.
  • Use only the payment options shown on the official website. Contact us if you receive suspicious payment instructions.
  • We will not mark an online payment as confirmed solely because a customer sends a screenshot; payment confirmation is checked against the provider's transaction records.
  • Do not attempt to access another customer's account or information. We may investigate suspicious activity and take proportionate steps to protect the service.
